METHODOLOGY / REPORT 01

Topographical Elevation Integration

We believe architectural structures should adapt to the topography, not overwrite it. By tracking natural slopes with high-fidelity coordinate mappings, our foundations embed gracefully into contours, mitigating massive soil disruption and preserving micro-drainage channels.

Slope Constraint < 35 degrees
Soil Anchorage Masonry Retainers
METHODOLOGY / REPORT 02

Active Hydrological Contouring

Water is the critical constraint of the arid Southwest. Our designs capture 100% of passive storm runoff via dry creek swales, natural retention pockets, and underground greywater infiltration lines, directing water to deep-root flora zones without active irrigation dependencies.

Runoff Capture 100% Passive
Infiltration Type Sub-surface Gravel
METHODOLOGY / REPORT 03

Ecosystem Restoration Indexes

Every structural footprint must balance with equivalent wild canvas creation. By planning density charts for native species like Saguaro, Prickly Pear, Velvet Mesquite, and Arizona White Oak, we rebuild native wildlife corridors within the urban and rural envelope.

Native Ratio > 90% Species
Canopy Coverage 40% Area Shade
